    The Lux Trail CF Di2 is Canyon’s answer to all-day trail riding: a full-carbon chassis, progressive geometry and Shimano Di2 electronic shifting that keeps every gear change clean when the trail gets steep. Built in our workshop and shipped ready to ride, it is the bike we recommend when you want one machine for climbs, flow and rocky descents.

    Up front a 150 mm fork and matching rear travel soak up roots and drops, while 29″ tubeless-ready wheels hold speed between corners. Hydraulic disc brakes, a dropper post and a cockpit dialled for long days on the mountain round out a spec that feels premium without making the bike hard to live with.
